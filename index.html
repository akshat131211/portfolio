Paper In Progress 2026 — Present

Phenology-Aware Crop Recommendation with GA-ANN and PhaT Under Strict No-Leak Evaluation

A reproducible crop recommendation benchmark integrating satellite-derived phenology features with genetic-algorithm-optimized neural networks and a custom phenology-aware transformer — evaluated under a strict group-disjoint, no-leak protocol against eight baseline models.

Overview

This project builds a crop recommendation benchmark and prediction pipeline using agronomic tabular features and satellite-derived phenology from the SICKLE dataset. The core contribution is a rigorous, reproducible evaluation framework that avoids the data leakage and inflated metrics common in agricultural ML literature.

Problem & Motivation

Many agricultural ML results are inflated by data leakage or unrealistic evaluation setups. This project focuses on reproducible benchmarking, phenology-aware feature integration from multiple satellite platforms, and fair comparison across classical ML and deep learning approaches.

Dataset & No-Leak Protocol

Built on the SICKLE dataset (sickle_dataset-002) with multimodal inputs: agronomic/context tabular features, engineered phenology from Sentinel-2, Sentinel-1, and Landsat-8, temporal sequence tensors, and optional pretrained UTAE-derived teacher features.

✓ Group-disjoint split by PLOT_ID
✓ Dropped leakage-prone columns (VARIETY, PADDY_BIN)
✓ No synthetic data (no SMOTE, no GANs)
✓ Consistent split policy across all models

Modeling Approach

GA-ANN

A PyTorch-based MLP (GAMLP) whose architecture and training hyperparameters are optimized by a custom genetic algorithm. Final predictions use a top-K ensemble of best GA candidates, with tree-guided distillation and appended out-of-fold teacher-probability features.

PhaT

Phenology-aware Transformer with a temporal encoder over phenology sequences, static tabular branch, day-of-year embedding, patch projection, auxiliary multitask heads, teacher distillation from strong tabular models, and a static shortcut classification head for robustness.

Interpretation

GA-ANN edges several strong tree baselines (LightGBM +0.19pp, Random Forest +0.16pp, XGBoost +0.22pp) on mean accuracy. However, CatBoost remains the strongest baseline at 63.99%. The margins between GA-ANN and the tree baselines are small and not statistically significant over 5 seeds (Wilcoxon p ≥ 0.0625). PhaT remains below the strongest tree baselines but demonstrates the viability of sequence-based phenology modeling. All results reflect honest, conservative evaluation.

Key Takeaways

  • Strict no-leak evaluation reveals realistic performance bounds — most models cluster in the 62–64% accuracy range on this challenging dataset.
  • GA-ANN achieves competitive accuracy through evolutionary architecture search without manual hyperparameter tuning.
  • Teacher distillation and probability-feature fusion provide measurable but modest improvements.
  • Rigorous evaluation methodology matters more than marginal accuracy gains.

Ongoing Research 2026 — Present

Seeing the Invisible: Satellite-Based Environmental Auditing of the Electric Vehicle Life Cycle

A dynamic, data-driven framework leveraging multi-sensor satellite data to monitor the real environmental impact of electric vehicles across mining, manufacturing, energy generation, and disposal stages — replacing static lifecycle assessments with real-time Earth observation.

Overview

While EVs are widely considered a sustainable alternative to traditional vehicles, their environmental footprint extends beyond zero tailpipe emissions. This project explores a next-generation approach using satellite-based monitoring systems to evaluate the true environmental cost across the entire EV lifecycle.

Motivation

Traditional static Life Cycle Assessments (LCA) often underestimate the upstream environmental impacts of EV supply chains. This project replaces static assumptions with real-time satellite observations, enabling transparent, verifiable, and policy-relevant environmental intelligence.

Dual-Pipeline Satellite Framework

Land Analysis Pipeline

Integrates Landsat and Sentinel-2 optical data with SAR radar to detect mining expansion, deforestation, and water resource depletion using satellite imagery and GIS tools.

Atmospheric Analysis Pipeline

Monitors pollutants like NO₂ and SO₂ using Sentinel-5P TROPOMI and Aura OMI data to estimate emissions from power plants and industrial processes linked to EV manufacturing.

Dynamic Impact Assessment

📡 Mining footprint expansion tracking
💧 Water resource depletion monitoring
🌫️ Air pollution trend analysis
⚡ Grid-based emissions from EV charging

Case Study: Global vs India

Compared EV adoption and environmental impacts across regions (India, China, EU, USA, MENA). Findings highlight how grid carbon intensity significantly influences EV sustainability — regions with carbon-intensive grids like India may see limited climate benefits from EV adoption.

Key Insights

  • EVs shift emissions upstream rather than eliminating them — mining, refining, and grid generation carry significant environmental costs.
  • Satellite data reveals hidden environmental costs often underestimated in traditional LCA models.
  • Real-time monitoring enables transparent, verifiable, and policy-relevant sustainability insights.
  • The long-term vision is automated environmental intelligence systems for policymakers and researchers.
